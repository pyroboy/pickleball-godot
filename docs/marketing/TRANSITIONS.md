# Transitions for every script

Which transition goes where in each reel, chosen for that reel's story. Every technique, how to
film or edit it, and the research behind it: `TRANSITION_TECHNIQUES.md`.

**In-camera / motion** (needs a real camera: you on camera, the over-the-shoulder phone, real-court
footage): **W** whip pan · **MC** match cut · **OW** object wipe · **HC** hand cover · **SR** spin
reveal · **PZ** punch-in.
**Storytelling / rhythm** (in the edit): **CA** cut on action · **BC** beat cut · **SC** smash
cut · **J** J-cut (sound first) · **L** L-cut (sound carries over) · **JC** jump cut · **SS** split
screen · **RW** rewind · **LS** loop seam · **PI** pattern interrupt · **DB** dip to black.

**Always:** hard cuts (CA) unless a row says otherwise; one or two visible transitions per reel,
6-12 frames each; nothing inside a gameplay clip (no ramps, freezes, fake moves, flashes); the
paddle pop or the bark lands exactly on the cut frame.

Times are reel seconds from each script. Scripts the audit marked CUT are listed last, in case
you keep them.

## Technique (T1-T10)

| Part | Transitions | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (the mistake) | Peak of the mistake first, **SC** to the explanation | The mistake is the scroll-stop |
| The rule | **L** your voice over the gameplay; **PI** one text line per 3-5 s | Teaching needs the picture to keep moving |
| Shown in the game | **CA** on the shot; **PZ** on the landing spot (kitchen) if small | The viewer must see where it lands |
| "In my game" | **W** or **OW** (paddle past the over-shoulder phone) to your thumb | Bridges the lesson to the game |
| End | "Save this." then **LS** to the mistake | Saves are the metric |
