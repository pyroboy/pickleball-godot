# Ate Joy

**Role:** Tindera of the sari-sari store; narrator of the B voice ("Kwento ni Ate Joy")  
**Where:** Hall end, behind the store counter

## What they really do in the game

- Stands behind the counter of the light-blue sari-sari store with the white window grille.
- Two watchers stand at her window.

## Personality (story only)

Mid-20s, sharp, kind, knows everyone's business and tells it with love. Sees the whole court from her window: the barangay's memory and its chismis desk. Never mean; she teases the people she likes.

## B-voice lines (draft: rewrite in your own Taglish)

- "Uy, may laro na naman sa court!"
- "Ako si Joy, sa sari-sari. Dito ko nakikita lahat."
- "Abangan n'yo sila. Marami pa akong kwento."

## Episode ideas

- Opener of every B reel: 1-2 s of her at the counter, then the character of the day.
- "Ate Joy's list": who in the crowd is really watching and who isn't.
- She never appears doing anything the game doesn't show: she narrates, others act.
