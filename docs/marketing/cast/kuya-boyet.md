# Kuya Boyet

**Role:** Malunggay pandesal vendor  
**Where:** Pandesal end (seen after the change of ends)

## What they really do in the game

- Drives in along the back lane on a motorcycle towing a bright green steel cabinet sidecar (wing doors, glass display, warmer drawers, canopy with signboard and jingle speaker).
- Stops, gets off, opens the doors; a queue forms; rolls come out of the warmer in a puff of steam; buyers walk off eating.
- Busy spells drift every 25-60 s while he's open.
- Spectators sometimes walk over to buy.
- Closes up, drives away, comes back later, taking turns with the jeepney.

## Personality (story only)

Early 40s, cheerful, punctual, proud of his pandesal. Knows every regular's order. The jingle is his entrance music.

## B-voice lines (draft: rewrite in your own Taglish)

- "Malunggay pandesal, mainit pa."
- "Bukas ulit, Kuya Boyet!"
