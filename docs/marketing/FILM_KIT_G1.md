# Film kit: G1 "I built it. It beats me."

The one top-5 reel only you can film: it has to be you playing and losing a point to your own
AI. Everything else is ready for it.

## Record (10-15 minutes)

1. iPhone: Settings > Control Center: add **Screen Recording**. Turn Do Not Disturb on.
2. Open Pixel Pickleball (build 102). Doubles, **Medium or Hard**.
3. Control Center > Screen Recording. Long-press it to turn the **microphone off** (game sound
   only).
4. Play a few games normally. Don't stage it: the hook only works if the loss is real.
5. Stop the recording. Note the rough time of 1-3 points you lost on a fast exchange (or won
   in a great one: that becomes a different hook).
6. Optional, the stronger version: a second phone filming over your shoulder (thumbs on the
   screen visible) at the same time. Start both recordings, then clap once in front of both:
   the clap lines the two up.
7. AirDrop the recording(s) to the Mac and tell me where they are and the rough times.

## What I do with it

- Find the point, cut it uncut from the fastest moment, short rewind, the whole point, the
  score after it, the next serve as the loop (transitions: `TRANSITIONS.md`, G1).
- Text: "I built it. It beats me." (only if true for that point), the score at the end.
- Measure the real numbers from the footage before they go on screen.
- Captions for the four apps, a cover, the beat sheet.

## Check before recording

- The phone has build 102 (the new look: slate court, outlined ball, HUD text on dark pills).
- Low Power Mode off (it caps the frame rate).
- Notifications off, battery above 30%.
