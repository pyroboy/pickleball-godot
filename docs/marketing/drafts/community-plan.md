# DRAFT: Community plan, "ka-barangay"

Status: draft (2026-11-25). Not reviewed.

The idea: the game's barangay and the real community are the same place. Followers are
**ka-barangay**; the more people join, the more the barangay in the game fills up. The story
(Kwento ng Court) is how they hear about it; joining is how they stay.

## 1. What followers can do

| Action | What they get | Where it shows | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Comment "sali" on a reel | Their first name on the next-game board, the scorer's table or the bulletin board | S2E7 and later reels | A place in the game that shows names (see `area-additions.md`, bulletin board) |
| Vote in a poll | Decide the story: next vendor (taho, sorbetes, balut), the puppies' names, who wins the rematch, the fiesta teams | The next episode | Nothing: polls in the apps |
| Send a photo of their real barangay court | "Court of the week": their court beside the game's court | A weekly reel or story | Their permission to post the photo |
| Join the beta (when it exists) | A spot in the crowd or the credits as a founding ka-barangay | In the game | A TestFlight plan |
| Bring their pickleball group | Feature their group playing the game | A reel | Everyone's OK on camera |

## 2. Words to use everywhere

- Followers: **ka-barangay**.
- A new follower: "Welcome sa barangay."
- Joining: "Sali ka" / "Comment 'sali'".
- The account bio: "Pixel Pickleball: a pickleball game on a barangay court. Ka-barangay, sali na."

## 3. The weekly rhythm

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| Mon | Kwento ng Court episode (Facebook first, then the others) |
| Wed | Gameplay or technique reel |
| Fri | Community: poll result, "Court of the week", or names joining the barangay |
| Daily | Reply to comments in the series' voice (Ate Joy can reply on the Facebook page) |

## 4. Where the community lives

- **Now:** the comment sections and a Facebook Page (Facebook is strong in the Philippines).
- **Later, once people ask:** a Facebook Group "Barangay Pixel Pickleball" for beta testers and
  court photos. Don't open an empty group; open it when comments ask "paano sumali?".
- Real pickleball groups and clubs that play on barangay courts: reach out once you have a
  playable beta to show them.

## 5. Rules

- **Consent first.** A name goes in the game only after the person says yes in a reply; first
  names only; remove anyone who asks, at once.
- **No minors' names or faces** without a parent's OK. Simplest: adults only.
- **No real officials, politics or religion** in the barangay; Kap and the tanod are fictional.
- **Polls must be honoured.** If the community votes taho, the next vendor is taho.
- **Never invent members, comments or votes.** No fake "500 ka-barangay joined" counts.
- **Moderate kindly:** delete insults about basketball players, regions or accents; the
  barangay welcomes everyone.

## 6. How to tell it's working

Track in `~/viral-reel-log/reels.json` and a simple tally: comments with "sali" per reel,
poll votes, court photos received, follows per 1,000 views on community posts vs story posts.
